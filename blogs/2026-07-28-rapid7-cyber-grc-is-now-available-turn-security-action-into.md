Compliance has become one of the biggest operational drains on modern security teams. CISOs are being asked to manage a growing sprawl of frameworks, prove control effectiveness more often, respond to more customer assurance requests, track risk across a growing web of third parties, and give executives and the board a clearer answer on whether cyber risk is actually going down. Most of that pressure does not come from the frameworks themselves.
