Today we’re proud to announce that Metasploit Framework version 6.5 has been released. Over the past two years, with the help of countless contributors, we’ve added 422 new modules along with a whole slew of new features. Malleable C2 Profiles for HTTP One of the latest and most requested features is support for Malleable C2 profiles across all current Meterpreter payloads.
